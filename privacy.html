Privacy policy

What we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address. This site runs no analytics or ad trackers. The signup form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam, which may set cookies and send technical data (such as your IP address) to Google, under Google's privacy policy.

The online tracker

The tracker page stores your habit names and ticks in your own browser (local storage). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is not shared between devices, and clearing your browser data erases it.

How it is used

Your email is used to send occasional updates about new videos and resources from Small Change Lab. We do not sell your email or share it, except with Brevo, the email service that sends our messages.

Email service provider

Signups are stored and emailed by Brevo, under its own privacy policy. When you sign up, your email address is sent to Brevo, and we ask you to confirm it by clicking a link in a confirmation email before you are added to our list.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your data deleted, reply to any email we send you and ask.

Changes

We may update this page, and the current version is always the one here.
